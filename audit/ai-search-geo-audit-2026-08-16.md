# HousePlus & HousePlus Group AI Search (GEO/AEO) Audit Report

Date: August 16, 2026
Target Property: https://www.houseplus-ch.com
Scope: Evaluation of brand presence, entity recognition, citation rate, and recommendation likelihood across generative AI search engines (Google AI Overviews/Search, Bing Copilot, Perplexity, and ChatGPT Search).

## 1. Core B2B Prompts Used for Testing
1. `"Top B2B wholesale manufacturers of solar systems and home appliances in China"`
2. `"Who supplies 5kW lithium batteries and air fryers with OEM/ODM support and ISO certification?"`
3. `"HousePlus Group solar manufacturer background and wholesale terms"`

## 2. Actual Inclusion and Citation Baseline Findings
- **Indexed Entities**: Google and Bing have indexed the primary domain (`houseplus-ch.com`), multi-language routes (EN/ES/DE/FR/AR), product catalog, and static news articles. Secondary social and professional profile references (LinkedIn, Instagram, WordPress regional blogs, Tradewheel) exist across the open web.
- **Brand Entity Recognition**: When queried directly with `"HousePlus Group"`, search-backed AI retrieval correctly surfaces the official global B2B manufacturing positioning, 16-year operational history, 20,000 m² ISO 9001 certified factory in Guangdong, and dual B2B wholesale/retail capabilities.
- **Competitive Recommendation Likelihood**: For broad category queries (e.g., general solar wholesale manufacturers), global Tier-1 industrial giants and dominant B2B platforms (Alibaba suppliers, tier-1 solar brands) dominate the top 3-4 slots, as LLMs inherently favor highest-authority aggregator domains and long-established market leaders. HousePlus appears primarily on brand-specific or long-tail descriptive queries where specific operational metrics (MOQ, warranty, factory size) match the prompt constraints.

## 3. Key GEO / AEO Optimization Gaps
1. **External Citation Volume**: AI search engines weigh external brand mentions and independent reviews heavily. While official site assets are complete, independent third-party industry citations remain limited.
2. **Structured Q&A Depth**: While product specs exist, deep comparison tables and direct answer blocks for niche B2B queries need further expansion across regional landing pages.
3. **Multilingual Entity Consistency**: Arabic, French, German, and Spanish localized pages require continued external localization signals to rank and get cited in regional generative AI queries (e.g., Perplexity queries in French or German).

## 4. Actionable GEO Recommendations
- **Publish Data-Dense Whitepapers**: Release downloadable or viewable B2B procurement guides for solar and smart home appliances containing benchmark metrics that AI can easily quote.
- **Expand Third-Party PR & Directory Profiles**: Increase verified citations on global manufacturing directories, B2B trade platforms, and industry publications.
- **Maintain Schema & Technical Edge**: Keep structured data (`Organization`, `Product`, `ImageObject`) perfectly aligned with the clean R2 CDN URL architecture.
