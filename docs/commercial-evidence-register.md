# Commercial Content Evidence Register

This register is the source-of-truth checklist for factual commercial and technical claims published on HousePlus pages. A claim must not be treated as verified until its evidence owner, document reference, validity date, and approved wording are recorded.

| Claim ID | Category | Claim or field | Affected route(s) | Evidence document / URL | Owner | Valid until | Approved wording | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CERT-001 | Certification | CE / FCC / RoHS certification | Product-specific pages | Add certificate number and issuing body | Product owner | Add date | Use only the scope covered by the certificate | pending |
| COMM-001 | Commercial term | MOQ | Product-specific pages | Add current quotation or supplier confirmation | Sales owner | Add date | State that MOQ is confirmed by quotation | pending |
| COMM-002 | Commercial term | Lead time | Product-specific pages | Add current production planning record | Operations owner | Add date | State that lead time depends on configuration and order | pending |
| SCALE-001 | Scale or performance | Factory, client, market or export scale | Company and factory pages | Add approved corporate evidence | Management owner | Add date | Use a dated, qualified statement | pending |
| SCALE-002 | Scale or performance | Product efficiency or technical performance | Product-specific pages | Add test report or datasheet | Engineering owner | Add date | Publish only the tested configuration and conditions | pending |

The current audit identified **276 evidence-review findings**: 184 certification-related, 34 commercial-term-related, and 58 scale or performance-related. The register should be expanded to one row per claim before those statements are treated as verified. Claims without evidence should use qualified wording such as “available upon request,” “subject to product configuration,” or “to be confirmed in the quotation.”

## Review rule

A release reviewer should reject new absolute claims when the evidence document, scope, validity date, or approved wording is missing. This register does not invent evidence and does not replace legal, certification, engineering, or commercial review.
